ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

A future vision grounded in decisions.

City 2049 is not a prediction and not a digital twin. It is a growing design-and-research playground: a way to connect operations research, autonomous systems, robotics and everyday observations inside one coherent future world.

The first setting is an original near-future city. Its atmosphere is inspired in part by Watch Dogs: Legion and its vision of a familiar urban world transformed by pervasive technology, while City 2049 uses original visual design, systems and scenarios.

CITY 2049 / MANIFESTO 001

The future city is a decision system.

We often imagine the future through objects: drones, robots, autonomous vehicles, intelligent buildings and satellites. City 2049 starts somewhere else — with the decisions connecting them.

Who goes where? Which resource is assigned? What should move first? How should a system respond when demand, weather, infrastructure or people change?

This project turns those questions into an explorable world. Each district is a story. Each story can become a model. Each model can become an experiment.
