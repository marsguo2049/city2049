# City 2049 — World Framework

## Core premise

City 2049 is a fictional near-future city used as a shared world for projects in operations research, AI, robotics and autonomous systems.

The project is deliberately positioned between two extremes:

- **not** a conventional “smart city dashboard” that only visualises indicators;
- **not** distant science fiction where technology has no connection to current engineering or decision problems.

The city should feel familiar enough that the transition from today to 2049 can be imagined.

## The first city

The initial setting is an original near-future city. A river and recognisable urban structure provide continuity with the present, while new operational layers are added on top. The atmosphere takes inspiration from **Watch Dogs: Legion**—especially the idea of familiar city life transformed by autonomous systems, drones, surveillance and algorithmic infrastructure—without recreating its map, story, factions or assets:

- autonomous road mobility,
- aerial robot fleets,
- intelligent vertical transport,
- multimodal freight and logistics,
- connected robotics,
- orbital / communication infrastructure,
- future energy and charging systems.

The project can later expand to other cities or wholly fictional regions without changing the underlying framework.

## The three layers of every City 2049 project

### 1. World layer
What is happening in the city? Who uses the system? Why does it matter in everyday life?

### 2. Decision layer
What decisions must be made? What are the objectives, constraints, uncertainty and interactions?

### 3. Method layer
How is the problem currently treated: exact optimisation, decomposition, heuristic, metaheuristic, simulation, rule-based logic, or visual demonstration?

The method layer must be described honestly. A visually impressive simulation is not automatically an optimiser.

## District framework

### City Core
Dense mixed-use buildings, vertical mobility, service systems and high-frequency demand.

**Current project:** Elevator Dispatch.

### Robotics / Research District
Multi-agent systems, robot coordination, autonomous experimentation and human–robot interaction.

**Current project:** Robot Soccer.

### Port & Logistics District
Freight terminals, multimodal transport, routing, consolidation, resilience and infrastructure planning.

**Future direction:** multimodal logistics and autonomous freight.

### Mobility Hub
Rail, autonomous vehicles, public transport, fleet dispatch and intermodal transfer.

**Future direction:** autonomous fleet and transport-network experiments.

### Airspace & Orbital Layer
Drone fleets, communications, sensing and infrastructure above the conventional street network.

**Current projects:** Drone Swarm and Orbital Network Design.

## Expansion rule

A new project belongs in City 2049 when it can answer all four questions:

1. What future everyday system does it represent?
2. What is the real decision problem?
3. What method is actually implemented?
4. What would a meaningful baseline or comparison look like?

This keeps the world imaginative without turning the portfolio into unsupported futuristic claims.

## Product evolution

### V1 — Explore
A visual world with interactive system nodes and clear conceptual framing.

### V2 — Simulate
City processes become dynamic: flows, agents, demand, events and disruptions.

### V3 — Optimise
Users can make or change decisions, run methods and compare outcomes.

### Longer-term possibility — Connected experiments
Individual repositories can expose small experiments that feed back into City 2049, making the city a shared interface for a family of OR / AI / robotics projects.
